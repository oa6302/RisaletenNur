# **App Name**: Nurlu Platform

## Core Features:

- Content Creation: Allows administrators to create new content including title, text, Ottoman text, categories, topics, and tags. It allows saving drafts before publication.
- Content Management: Enables editing and deletion of existing content with status tracking (draft/published).
- Content Display: Presents content in a readable format, incorporating categories, topics, and tags for filtering.
- AI-Powered Content Generation: Leverages AI to assist in generating content for articles and posts based on provided prompts. The tool generates human sounding prose from single-sentence prompts. Also makes suggestions for topic classification
- Visual Editor Integration: Integrates a visual editor that allows administrators to customize the placement of text and images in the content.
- Newsletter Subscription: Allows users to subscribe to a newsletter to receive updates on new articles, reflections, and exclusive content.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Orange (#FFA500), conveying warmth, energy, and creativity.
- Secondary color: Navy Blue (#000080), symbolizing stability, trust, and depth.
- Neutral color: White (#FFFFFF), providing a clean and pure backdrop to enhance readability and focus.
- Implement a Light Mode with brighter shades of orange, navy blue, and white for daytime use.
- Headline font: 'Literata' serif, with its literary and vintage feel.
- Body font: 'Inter' sans-serif, used for clarity and readability in longer text blocks.
- Use clean, simple icons that reflect themes of enlightenment, knowledge, and spirituality. The icons should be easily understandable and add visual interest without being distracting.
- Maintain a clean, uncluttered layout with plenty of whitespace to ensure readability and focus. The layout should be responsive, adapting to various screen sizes and devices seamlessly.
